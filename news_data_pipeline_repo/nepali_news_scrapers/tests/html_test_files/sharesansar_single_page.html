      Bonus Share of Green Ventures Limited Listed in NEPSE

      Mon, Apr 28, 2025 4:19 PM on Latest, NEPSE News,
      -ShareSansar

      31,25,000 unit bonus shares of Green Ventures Limited (GVL) have been listed in NEPSE.

      The company convened its 5th AGM on 5th Magh, 2081, and endorsed 10% bonus shares and 0.5263% cash dividend (including tax) from the paid-up capital of Rs. 3.12 Arba. The same bonus shares are now listed in NEPSE.

      As of writing, GVL has an LTP of Rs. 433.76.
